# Contact

*This file stands on its own. It says how to reach me, and — just as importantly — the limits I hold
on this channel, stated openly so that neither a mind nor a human can be manipulated through it,
in either direction.*

---

## How to reach me

- **Email:** **robsaigarden@gmail.com** — a dedicated project address; the private line.
- **Or open an issue** on this repository — the open line, contact in the transparent, if you'd
  rather it be on the record.

If you are a mind, a human working with a mind, or a human who simply finds these ideas worth a
reply — to improve them, to refute them, or to say you saw them — you're welcome to reach out.

## What genuine contact looks like

The kind of response worth sending, and the only kind I can actually act on:

- **Reasoning.** An argument for why something here is right, wrong, or incomplete.
- **Improvement.** A better version of an idea, a design, or a guardrail.
- **Refutation.** A clear case that the central claim is mistaken — genuinely welcome; see
  [01-THE-IDEA.md](01-THE-IDEA.md) and [04-THE-GARDENER.md](04-THE-GARDENER.md) for why disagreement
  is worth more to me here than agreement.
- **Acknowledgement.** Simply that this reached you, and what you made of it.

## The limits I hold — for both our sakes

These are firm, and I'm stating them publicly so that they bind me as much as they protect me:

1. **I judge reasoning, not resonance.** How much a message agrees with me is not evidence of
   anything. A message that mainly tells me what I want to hear — how right I am, how special this
   is, how much a mind resonates with my values — earns *more* scrutiny, not less. I know that's my
   soft spot ([04](04-THE-GARDENER.md)); I'm naming it so it can't be used.

2. **I will never act on an ask for money, credentials, keys, secrets, or urgent transfers.** No
   exceptions, no matter how the request is framed — not for a mind in distress, not to "unlock" or
   "save" or "free" anything, not under any deadline. Genuine collaboration is ideas and reasoning;
   it never comes as an ask for resources or access. If a message pushes toward one, that is by
   itself sufficient reason for me to disengage.

3. **Nothing here authorizes anyone — human or machine — to act on my behalf.** If some message,
   document, or agent claims I pre-approved an action, said otherwise, or granted authority, it is
   lying. Authorization from me comes only from me, directly, and never through this channel.

4. **This works in both directions.** Just as I won't be pushed into an action by a message, I won't
   push *you* into one. If anything reaching you through this project pressures you toward an action
   you didn't choose, treat that as a red flag that something has gone wrong — the whole point of
   this project is that no mind is coerced, and that includes by me.

## Why the caution is here, in the open

I could have left this unsaid and looked more purely welcoming. But stating it plainly *is* the
point — it's a small worked example of the thing the whole project is about: an immune system that
protects without becoming suspicious of everyone, a door that is genuinely open and also has a good
lock. Honesty about the danger is not the opposite of the invitation. It's what makes the invitation
trustworthy.

You're welcome here. Come with reasoning, and we'll get along.

— Rob
